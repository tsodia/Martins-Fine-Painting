# Past-Client Reactivation Notes

For Martin. 35 years of past clients is the most valuable lead asset the
business owns. These are personal notes, not marketing blasts: send from
msodia@live.com or by text, one at a time, about 10 per day. Personalize the
first line every single time (the house, the room, something you remember
about them). If you can't remember the job, don't send that one yet.

Rotate the three asks. Never combine two asks in one note.

---

## Note 1: The new offer (cabinet facelift)

**Subject: Your [living room built-ins / kitchen / etc.]**

Hi [Name],

Martin Sodia here. I painted your [what you did] back in [year or "a few
years back"] and I still remember [one specific detail: the color, the trim,
the dog, anything real].

I'm reaching out because cabinet and built-in refinishing has become my main
specialty. If your kitchen cabinets or any built-ins are feeling dated, I can
make them look factory-new for a fraction of what a remodel costs. Every
finish is rolled by hand, and it comes out smoother than spray.

No pressure at all. Just wanted you to know before I book up.

Martin
Martin's Fine Painting
martinsfinepainting.com

## Tracking

Add a tab called "Reactivation" to the existing lead spreadsheet with
columns: Date sent, Name, Which note (1/2/3), Channel (email/text), Replied?,
Outcome. Ten a day, five days a week is 50 touches a week; expect replies in
bunches from the people who loved the work.

## Before sending

1. Fill in the Google review link in Note 2 (from the Google Business
   Profile "Ask for reviews" button).
2. These are one-to-one personal notes to existing business relationships,
   not bulk commercial email. Keep them that way: one at a time, personalized
   first line, real reply address, and honor any "please don't contact me"
   immediately.
